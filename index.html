Get product updates
Does FaraiOS host my business website?

Yes. You can publish your business pages on FaraiOS. Standalone hosting packages, custom-domain options and their pricing are not yet confirmed.

Can I manage bookings in FaraiOS?

You can create and schedule bookings in your business workspace and assign staff. Public online booking intake is not enabled in the current live release.

Which businesses is FaraiOS designed for?

FaraiOS is being developed for service businesses, with cleaning, beauty and wellness, and maintenance and trades as pilot examples. Specialist industry workflows are still being developed; an industry choice does not mean every requirement is supported.

How much does FaraiOS cost?

FaraiOS is currently free to use in its prototype stage. Future paid subscriptions, standalone hosting packages and custom-domain pricing have not been announced. See current pricing.

Can customers pay online?

The current release supports invoices and manual records of payments you have received. Online payment collection and accounting integrations are not included.

Can I update my website after publishing?

Yes. Update your branding, images, services, contact details and page content. Save and preview a draft before publishing your changes. SEO controls help describe your pages; search rankings are not guaranteed.
