Current pricing status

FaraiOS is currently free to use in its prototype stage. No paid subscription plan is currently offered. Future paid subscriptions, standalone hosting packages and custom-domain options have not been announced.

For help getting started, contact hello@faraios.com.

Discuss what you need

Tell us your business type, number of people, website needs and the work you want to manage. We can discuss the current fit and which requirements need further development.

If pricing changes

If paid services are introduced later, confirm the full price, billing period, included features, limits and cancellation terms before agreeing.

Business service prices are separate

Free use of FaraiOS does not make a listed business’s services free. Prices charged by a business using FaraiOS are set by that business. Ask the named business about its service quote, booking, payment or cancellation arrangements.
