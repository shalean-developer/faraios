About FaraiOS

FaraiOS brings a business website and everyday service administration into one workspace. Business owners can publish their pages, respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, schedule work, assign staff and keep invoices and manual payment records organised.

Built for service businesses

Our starting examples are cleaning, beauty and wellness, and maintenance and trades. FaraiOS is being developed around shared business needs; specialist industry workflows are still being developed.

Your website and the work behind it

Owners manage their branding, service descriptions, images, coverage and contact information. Published business pages are hosted on FaraiOS. Draft content stays separate from published content.

Customers deal with the named service business about its services, quotes, bookings and invoices. FaraiOS provides the platform; the listed business provides its services.

Clear about availability

The current release supports internal booking management and manual payment records. Public online booking intake, online payment collection, accounting integrations and automated marketing are not included in the live offer. FaraiOS is currently free to use in its prototype stage. Future paid subscriptions, standalone hosting packages and custom-domain options have not been announced.
