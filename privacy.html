Waitlist privacy notice.

This notice covers the FaraiOS waitlist. Contact hello@faraios.com with questions, correction requests or deletion requests.

What we collect

Your name, email address, business type and selected interests. We also record when you requested and confirmed updates, the consent wording and version, and any withdrawal.

Why we use it

To confirm your request and send FaraiOS waitlist, launch and early-access updates relevant to your interests. Joining does not create a product account or guarantee a launch date, service availability or pricing. We do not sell your waitlist details.

Your choice

The consent box is unchecked by default. You are added to the active waitlist only after confirming your email. Keep your confirmation email: its unsubscribe link also works after you confirm. You can also contact us to withdraw, correct or delete your details. If you unsubscribe and later want to rejoin, contact us.

Where details are held

Details are stored in private server storage on our Plesk hosting service and accessed by the FaraiOS owner through hosting controls. Our hosting and email infrastructure process data to operate the waitlist. Hosting backups may retain copies until their normal expiry.

How long we keep them

Unconfirmed requests are removed after seven days. Withdrawn records are kept for up to 30 days for administration, then deleted. Confirmed entries expire after 12 months unless you join again with fresh consent. Routine queue records expire after 30 days. These periods are applied by the daily maintenance task. Owner exports are private working copies and must follow the same retention periods.

Spam checks and cookies

We use form validation, a hidden bot field, timing checks and rate limits. The application stores a temporary keyed hash of your connection address for rate limiting, rather than the raw address. Hosting security/access logs may separately record connection information under the host’s retention settings. A necessary session cookie protects the form; the waitlist adds no advertising or tracking cookies.

Email delivery

Confirmation emails are sent through our hosting mail service. Your request stays saved if email sending is temporarily unavailable. Messages may be delayed or filtered by your email provider.
