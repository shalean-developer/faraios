Who handles your information

FaraiOS operates the platform, account access, security and its own waitlist. Contact hello@faraios.com about these activities or for help directing a privacy request.

When you contact or request a service from a business on FaraiOS, that business decides how to use your details to respond and provide its service. FaraiOS stores and processes those service records on the business’s behalf. The business is responsible for its service-related use of your information, including information it keeps outside FaraiOS. FaraiOS separately handles information needed to operate and protect the platform.

The business name and published contact details appear on its website. Contact that business first about its service records; if you cannot reach it, contact FaraiOS for assistance. This notice does not replace a business’s own privacy information or authorise it to use your details for unrelated purposes.

Accounts

Creating an account requires your name, email address and password. Supabase Auth manages authentication and password security. We store your business name, type, country, time zone and ownership link when you set up a business. Without the required details, we cannot create or operate your account.

We use this information to verify your email, sign you in, save your settings and help recover access. Verification and password-reset emails are account security messages sent through Resend. Creating an account does not join the waitlist or subscribe you to marketing.

Only business details you explicitly publish are intended to appear on the public business website. Saving a draft does not publish it. Review public contact details and images before publishing.

Enquiries and quote requests

The enquiry form collects your name, email address and/or phone number, your selected service if supplied, and what you need. Your name, at least one contact method and the requested description are required so the selected business can respond.

FaraiOS stores the submitted enquiry privately for that business owner. The business uses it to answer questions, discuss requirements and prepare a quote. It is not posted publicly or shared with other businesses through the public page. An enquiry does not create a confirmed booking, a FaraiOS account or marketing consent.

Contact the business to discuss or correct an enquiry. Avoid including identity numbers, payment-card details, health information or other sensitive information in the free-text field.

Booking requests

The booking flow collects your selected service, rooms or extras where offered, service address and area, preferred date and time, name, email address and/or phone number, and optional unit details or notes. A service, address, preferred time and the required contact details are needed to request a visit.

When you send the request, FaraiOS stores these details, the service and price snapshot, and the business time zone privately for the selected business owner to review. The business uses them to assess the request, contact you and arrange the service. A request is pending until the business confirms it; no payment is taken by this request form. Requesting a booking does not subscribe you to marketing.

Before submission, the flow can keep a draft in this browser tab to restore your progress after a reload. See Cookies and browser storage. Opening a privacy link does not send the booking request. Do not put passwords, access codes or sensitive personal information in your notes; arrange any essential access details directly with the business.

Business records

Business owners can keep customer and staff contact details, quotes, bookings, assignments, invoices and manual payment records in FaraiOS. These records support their service administration and are separate from a public business profile. The business must provide accurate privacy information for the details it collects or enters, and decide what it needs to retain.

Public website visitors do not receive access to these private records. FaraiOS uses ownership controls to separate businesses’ records. Authorised platform administration and service providers may process information as needed to operate, secure and support the service. Ask the business about who it authorises to handle your information and any copies it holds elsewhere.

Waitlist updates

What we collect and why

The separate waitlist collects your name, email address, business type and selected interests. It records the request, confirmation, consent wording and version, and any withdrawal. We use this information to confirm your request and send FaraiOS waitlist, launch and early-access updates relevant to your interests.

Your choice

Joining is optional. The marketing-consent box starts unchecked, and you join the active waitlist only after confirming your email. Joining does not create a product account or guarantee a launch date, availability or pricing. We do not sell your waitlist details.

Keep your confirmation email: its unsubscribe link also works after confirmation and can cancel an unconfirmed request. You can also contact hello@faraios.com to withdraw, correct or request deletion. If you unsubscribe and later want to rejoin, contact us.

Email delivery and retention

Resend sends confirmation emails and handles delivery status. Confirmed details are also sent to the FaraiOS owner for waitlist administration. Bounced or complained-about addresses are suppressed. Sending can be retried if a provider is temporarily unavailable.

The waitlist cleanup rules remove unconfirmed requests after seven days, retain withdrawn or suppressed records for up to 30 days, and expire confirmed entries after 12 months unless there is fresh consent. Routine queue records expire after 30 days. Cleanup depends on the maintenance task running; provider logs, backups and private exports have separate handling. These waitlist periods do not apply to account or business service records.

Cookies and browser storage

Necessary session cookies support sign-in and protect forms. Account access and refresh tokens stay on the server; the account cookie holds a session identifier. The account session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.

The booking flow uses this tab’s session storage for an unfinished draft, including entered contact and address details. It restores only drafts saved within the last 30 minutes. Expiry is checked when the flow is opened again, rather than by a background deletion timer. The draft is cleared after a successful request; your browser controls tab-storage retention. Do not use a shared device for sensitive details.

Spam protection uses form checks, timing checks, temporary connection hashes and rate limits. Hosting and authentication security logs may separately record connection information. These forms do not add advertising or marketing-tracking cookies. Opening the privacy notice does not change your consent choices.

Providers and retention

FaraiOS uses Supabase for authentication and stored records, Plesk-managed website hosting, and Resend for relevant emails. The current Supabase project is hosted in Ireland. Hosting, email, security logs and backups may involve processing in other countries.

Account information is kept while your account is active. Enquiries and service records remain available for the business’s follow-up and administration; they do not currently have an automatic deletion period matching the waitlist. Ask the business or FaraiOS about access, correction or deletion. We will explain where records need to be retained for service administration, legal obligations or dispute handling. Logs, backups and copies outside the platform can have different retention periods.

Your choices and privacy requests

Contact the business about its customer or service records, or hello@faraios.com about your FaraiOS account, waitlist or platform privacy concern. You can ask what information is held, request access or correction, request deletion, or object to processing where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing private information.

Waitlist withdrawal stops those marketing updates; it does not delete a product account or cancel a service booking. To change a booking or enquiry, contact the business. Removing a browser draft does not remove a request already submitted.

If a privacy concern remains unresolved, you can contact South Africa’s Information Regulator for information about complaints. Other rights may apply depending on your location and circumstances.
