## Durable integrity and access

private_t22.agreements and accruals have RLS enabled and no direct anon/authenticated/service_role table access. Only the authenticated invoker RPC delegates to an empty-search-path private function. It checks live sessions, locks current business/membership, and obtains business scope before records. Owner writes; owner/manager reads. Staff own-source adjustment reviews remain available through existing RLS; a new cleaner portal is T24, not this increment.

Agreement uniqueness is business/booking. Exact request and original actor retries return the existing agreement; altered payload/identity rejects. Saved rules are checked directly and never accepted from a submitted rule object. Assignment, version, current membership, service, duration, protected recurring kind, explicit amounts, overflow and evidence validation fail closed.

Accrual locks booking then agreement, verifies completion/team/duration and independent entitlement, advances an agreement revision to fence stale snapshots, and records accrual plus all worker sources in one transaction. One bad/colliding source rolls back the entire attempt. Original worker source IDs are generated once and retained in the agreement. A different retry request cannot create a second accrual. Existing historical synthetic sources force reconciliation rather than overwrite.

A zero lead and a positive worker remain independent lines. Subsequent disputes/corrections use separate worker journals; they cannot erase or redirect another worker's original amount. Reimbursements are displayed separately. No automatic fines, damage/refund/nonpayment deductions or payment-driven accrual.
