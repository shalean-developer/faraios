## Scope and accepted behaviour

Earnings rules → Supervisor allowances lists active staff accounts and preserves history for former staff. The owner identifies which staff member has an agreed supervisor role; this setting does not grant a role or business access. Each business has separate settings, including for shared staff. There is no Shalean-specific or universal R1,000 default. No saved change means Off.

An active owner can schedule On with a positive Rand amount, or Off with zero, from a future calendar month. The earliest month is the next month in the business settings time zone (UTC if unconfigured). Changes require a reason describing the agreed terms. Amounts use integer cents. A full monthly allowance is additional to booking earnings, not part of the percentage pool or visit cap. No proration, penalty, deductions or pay-law determination is introduced.

Every save appends a version, actor, server timestamp, effective month and reason. Rows cannot be inserted, updated or deleted by application clients directly. The configuration RPC verifies current active owner and target staff memberships, scope, future month and expected version. Concurrent saves serialize per business/staff pair; stale saves fail without replacing another decision. The audit event commits atomically with the change. Managers can read and preview their business; staff and foreign businesses cannot access these configuration records. Own-earnings portal access is a separate future capability.

For a selected month, the preview uses the most recent effective month at or before it, then the latest version for that effective month. An Off change persists until another scheduled On. A later version for an earlier future month does not cancel a separately scheduled later change; the complete schedule is shown. Earlier records and past/current periods cannot be rewritten using the toggle. Existing accepted terms still require their own review; a dashboard setting is not a contract amendment.

The monthly preview shows booking earnings, monthly allowance and combined preview separately. Booking earnings are an explicit optional planning input, not a verified earned ledger. Blank booking earnings remain unknown, not zero. Overflow is rejected. The page does not accrue liabilities, freeze periods, approve payouts or send transfers. Existing bookings, recurring agreements, service rules, prices, invoices and accounting remain untouched.
